## Before shipping

- Packs marked "None included" need their license/source confirmed before any release.
- `modern-tiles-free` is non-commercial; the full paid version is a separate license.
- The WAVs are 24-bit stereo 44.1 kHz, 155-213 s each, ~180 MB total. Convert to OGG for the app and consider Git LFS or keeping originals out of the repo.
