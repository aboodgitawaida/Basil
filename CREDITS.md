## Drawn for this project

The kitchen itself (walls, floor, cabinets, worktop, window, the view, the
table and chairs, the lamps), the title-screen landscape, the open fridge and
freezer doors, Basil's awake poses, the egg, the egg, pasta, rice and salad
dishes, the clock hands and the site icon are drawn in code or generated by
the scripts in `tools/` from the packs above. Every sprite is remapped onto
one palette so the packs read as one set.
