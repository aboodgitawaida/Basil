## Drawn for this project

The kitchen itself (walls, floor, cabinets, worktop, window, the view, the
table and chairs, the lamps), the title-screen landscape, the open fridge and
freezer doors, Basil's awake poses, the clock hands and the site icon are drawn
in code or generated by the scripts in `tools/` from the packs above.

## In `assets/` but not used

These raw packs are kept for reference and are not part of the built app:
Modern Tiles (LimeZu, free version, non-commercial only), Cryo's Mini GUI and
items, the Cozy Cute UI sheet, a controller-button atlas, social buttons, and
two ambience tracks.
